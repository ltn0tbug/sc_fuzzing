## Stat guardrails
rank-based only (Spearman/Mann-Whitney/Wilcoxon — heavy right-tailed features) · effect sizes
(rank-biserial, ρ) before p (N≈32/cell, low power) · bootstrap CIs (1000) on ρ · keep censored
time-to-bug rows (KM, ~85% censoring) · **stratify by dataset** (SmartBugs ~33 LOC/~2 DeFi calls vs
DeFiHackLabs ~494 LOC/~22 — not exchangeable) · no held-out ML claims at N=115 (PCA qualitative).

## Findings (6-method run, FinanceFuzz integrated 2026-06-29 — full SmartBugs N=33; supersedes the partial-17 run)
1 **Complexity flip (headline):** on complex DeFiHackLabs random/RL > LLM (RLFuzz 28%/0.455 cov; SSCFuzz
24%/0.410); on simple SmartBugs the guided order inverts — SSCFuzz 42% & LLMFuzz 39% ≫ RLFuzz 21% &
MADFuzz 24% detection (RandomFuzz tops both at 28%/48%, tied on SB by FinanceFuzz 48%; full set is harder
than the easy-17 — 6-method union solvable 26/33, 17/33 among generation methods; FinanceFuzz's differential
oracle cracks 9 reentrancy PoCs the rest miss, wins SB reentrancy 10/18). · 2 RL rescues the LLM: SSCFuzz vs LLMFuzz
+9.8pp cov on DeFi (δ=0.34, p<0.001) AND +7.5pp on SB (δ=0.43, p=0.0002); SSCFuzz beats RLFuzz detection
on SB (McNemar p=0.039). · 3 difficulty = structural complexity: coverage↓ with cyclomatic_complexity
(ρ=−0.67 DeFi), solvability↓ with size on SB (loc ρ=−0.69), NOT defi_action_count. · 4 MADFuzz outcome
~independent of features (MI top 0.09) while LLM/SSC/random are structure-sensitive (MI top 0.17–0.29);
RLFuzz now moderate (0.19) — explains the flip. · 5 depth ≠ reasoning: deep call seqs find FEWER bugs
(Fisher OR<1 all methods; now significant on SB too — SSCFuzz OR=0.09 p=0.021); win comes from arg/caller
selection not sequence length. · 6 cost: continuous-LLM ~65–227× less token-efficient than MADFuzz
one-shot seed. Full writeups in `research/experiment_analysis*.md`.
